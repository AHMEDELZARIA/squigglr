\section{MIS of the Audio Output Module (\mref{mAudio})}
\label{sec:mAudio}

\subsection{Module}

AudioOutput

\subsection{Uses}

None.

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
play & $s$: SoundIdT & -- & -- \\
stop & -- & -- & -- \\
setMuted & $m$: $\mathbb{B}$ & -- & -- \\
isMuted & -- & $\mathbb{B}$ & -- \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{muted}$: $\mathbb{B}$, whether sound is turned off for the
active learner.
\item $\mathit{playing}$: $\mathbb{B}$, whether a sound is playing.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{speaker}$: SoundIdT $\cup$ $\{\bot\}$, the sound the browser's
audio output on the device is playing, or $\bot$ when it plays none.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item The caller (the Child Screen Module, \mref{mChildScreen}) calls
\texttt{setMuted} with the negation of the active learner's audio setting
(audio on means not muted) from the Learner Settings Module (\mref{mSettings})
before it plays any sound for that learner,
and again whenever the active learner or the setting changes (NFR-UX-010).
\item Every sound is also shown on screen as a message, so a sound that the
browser does not play loses no information (NFR-UX-021).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

The full semantics are given in Revision~0. In brief: \texttt{play} plays $s$
on the speaker unless $\mathit{muted}$, stopping any sound already playing;
\texttt{stop} stops the sound that is playing, so that a long sound can be
stopped from the screen it plays on (NFR-UX-022); \texttt{setMuted} sets
$\mathit{muted}$, and muting also stops the sound that is playing;
\texttt{isMuted} gives $\mathit{muted}$.

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
