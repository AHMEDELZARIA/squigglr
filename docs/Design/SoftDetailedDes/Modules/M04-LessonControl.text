\section{MIS of the Guided Lesson Control Module (\mref{mLessonControl})}
\label{sec:mLessonControl}

\subsection{Module}

LessonControl

\subsection{Uses}

Input Device Module (\mref{mInputDevice}), Lesson Catalogue Module
(\mref{mCatalogue}), Attempt Module (\mref{mAttempt}), Improvement Measure
Module (\mref{mImprovement}), Attempt Record Module (\mref{mRecord}), Adult
Access Module (\mref{mAccess}), Learner Settings Module (\mref{mSettings}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

\begin{itemize}
\item StatusT $= \{\mathit{noLesson}, \mathit{active}, \mathit{suggested},
\mathit{ended}\}$, the session status $q$ of SRS Section~9.2, with
$\mathit{noLesson}$ before the first lesson is chosen.
\item ReportT = tuple of $(\mathit{feedback}: \text{seq of FeedbackKindT},
\mathit{difficulties}: \text{set of DifficultyT})$, what the child is told
after an event, in the order it is given. The difficulties are those of the
attempt that just ended.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
select & $a$: AdultIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT, $\ell$: LessonIdT, $l$: StageT & -- &
adultCheckFailed, unsupportedDevice, unknownLesson, notOwner, unavailable \\
beginPractice & -- & -- & notAtDemonstration \\
addStroke & $s$: StrokeT & ReportT & noAttempt \\
tick & -- & ReportT & -- \\
answerSuggestion & $a$: AdultIdT, $\mathit{accept}$: $\mathbb{B}$, $l$:
DrawingStageT & -- & adultCheckFailed, noSuggestion, notEarlierStage \\
stop & -- & -- & -- \\
status & -- & StatusT & -- \\
lesson & -- & LessonIdT & noLesson \\
stage & -- & StageT & noLesson \\
furthest & -- & StageT & noLesson \\
figure & -- & FigureT & noLesson \\
demonstration & -- & seq of PathT & noLesson \\
guide & -- & GuideT & noLesson, notAtDrawingStage \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

The state follows the state $\sigma$ of SRS Section~9.2:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\ell$: LessonIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT and $l$: StageT, the lesson, the
learner and the current stage.
\item $q$: StatusT, initially $\mathit{noLesson}$.
\item $n_f$, $n_a$, $k$: $\mathbb{N}_0$, the failed attempts, the evaluable
attempts and the current failure streak at this stage.
\item $H$: seq of $\mathbb{R}$, the stage results of the evaluable attempts at
this stage, in order.
\item $s$: $\mathbb{B}$, whether a go-back suggestion is pending, and $i$:
$\mathbb{N}_0$, its index in the lesson record.
\item $z$: $\mathbb{B}$ and $x$: $\mathbb{N}_0$, whether the extension has been
granted at this stage, and the evaluable attempts made since.
\item $f$: StageT, the furthest stage reached in the lesson.
\item $\mathit{set}$: SettingsT, the learner's adult-set values when the lesson
was chosen.
\item $\mathit{att}$: AttemptT, the current attempt, if there is one.
\item $r$: LessonRecordIdT, the lesson record of this run of the lesson.
\item $\mathit{prompted}$: $\mathbb{B}$, whether the idle check-in is showing.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

None. The time of the child's last input is read from the Input Device Module
(\mref{mInputDevice}).

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item The Child Screen Module (\mref{mChildScreen}) passes each finished stroke
to \texttt{addStroke}, calls \texttt{tick} about once a second while a lesson
is showing, and shows each ReportT with the Feedback Module (\mref{mFeedback}).
\item The Adult View Module (\mref{mAdultView}) calls \texttt{stop} before the
adult switches learner or ends the session.
\item One lesson runs at a time on a device.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

The full semantics are given in Revision~0, as a transition for each row of the
SRS table of guided lesson state transitions (SRS Table~\ref{tab:lrn-transitions}).
In brief:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{select} runs the adult check of the Adult Access Module for
$\mathit{lessonChoice}$ and the device check of the Input Device Module, ends any lesson in progress as
unfinished, reads the learner's settings, starts a new lesson record, and sets
$(\ell, l)$ with all stage counts, the history, the suggestion and the
extension reset (AdultSelect; FR-LRN-016, FR-LRN-018, SAF-RAY-001,
SAF-RAY-002). It raises \texttt{notOwner} if $p$ is not one of $a$'s
learners, and \texttt{unavailable} if the learner's settings cannot be read.
\item \texttt{beginPractice} moves from stage $D$ to stage $T$ and starts the
first attempt.
\item \texttt{addStroke} gives the stroke to the current attempt. If the
attempt ends, it saves the attempt record, updates the counts, and applies the
first of these that holds, in this order: a pass moves to the next stage or
completes the lesson (FR-LRN-023); the end-at-limit rule ends the lesson
(FR-LRN-021, SAF-RAY-005); the extension rule grants the one extension
(FR-LRN-020, SAF-RAY-006); a go-back suggestion is offered if
SuggestBack holds (FR-LRN-019). Otherwise a new attempt starts at the same
stage (FR-LRN-015). The report gives the feedback for what happened.
\item \texttt{tick} lets an open attempt end after \texttt{ATTEMPT\_IDLE}, with
the same rules as \texttt{addStroke}, shows the check-in after
\texttt{LESSON\_IDLE\_PROMPT} with no input, and ends the lesson after
\texttt{LESSON\_IDLE\_END} (SAF-RAY-009).
\item \texttt{answerSuggestion} runs the adult check for
$\mathit{suggestionAnswer}$. Declining clears $s$ and resets $k$, and $l$ is not
used; accepting moves to the earlier stage $l$. Both are saved in the
lesson record (FR-REC-010). A suggestion that is never answered stays pending
and blocks nothing.
\item \texttt{stop} interrupts the current attempt and ends the lesson as
unfinished (FR-REC-005, FR-REC-007).
\item The other programs give the state, and the figure, demonstration and
guide of the current lesson and stage from the Lesson Catalogue.
\end{itemize}
A pending suggestion that a pass, \texttt{select} or the end of the lesson
clears is saved as ignored (FR-REC-010).

In every state, $n_a \leq \texttt{ATTEMPT\_LIMIT\_MAX}$, $0 \leq x \leq

(invariant I5 of SRS Section~9.2).

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

These are defined in Revision~0, from SRS Section~9.2:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{suggestBack}$: $\mathbb{B}$, the predicate SuggestBack.
\item $\mathit{atLimit}$: $\mathbb{B}$, the guard of the end-at-limit rule.
\item $\mathit{canExtend}$: $\mathbb{B}$, the guard of the extension rule.
\item $\mathit{next}$: $\{T, E\} \rightarrow \{E, I\}$, the next stage.
\end{itemize}
