\section{MIS of the Lesson Catalogue Module (\mref{mCatalogue})}
\label{sec:mCatalogue}

\subsection{Module}

LessonCatalogue

\subsection{Uses}

Storage Module (\mref{mStorage}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None. The lessons are data, read at run time, so that a lesson can be added,
changed or removed without a change to the code (NFR-MAINT-001, LC-001).

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

\begin{itemize}
\item CategoryT $= \{\mathit{line}, \mathit{shape}, \mathit{simpleDrawing}\}$,
the categories of the approved catalogue (FR-LRN-001).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
load & -- & -- & catalogueUnavailable, badLesson \\
lessons & -- & seq of LessonIdT & notLoaded \\
name & $\ell$: LessonIdT & string & notLoaded, unknownLesson \\
category & $\ell$: LessonIdT & CategoryT & notLoaded, unknownLesson \\
figure & $\ell$: LessonIdT & FigureT & notLoaded, unknownLesson \\
demonstration & $\ell$: LessonIdT & seq of PathT & notLoaded, unknownLesson \\
guide & $\ell$: LessonIdT, $l$: DrawingStageT & GuideT & notLoaded,
unknownLesson \\
difficulties & $\ell$: LessonIdT, $l$: DrawingStageT & set of DifficultyT &
notLoaded, unknownLesson \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{loaded}$: $\mathbb{B}$, whether the catalogue has been read,
initially false.
\item $\mathit{catalogue}$: seq of LessonT, the approved lessons in the order
they are offered, where LessonT is the local type below.
\end{itemize}

\noindent LessonT = tuple of $(\mathit{id}: \text{LessonIdT},$
$\mathit{name}: \text{string},$ $\mathit{category}: \text{CategoryT},$
$\mathit{figure}: \text{FigureT},$ $\mathit{demonstration}: \text{seq of
PathT},$ $\mathit{difficulties}: \text{DrawingStageT} \rightarrow \text{set of
DifficultyT})$.

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

None. The stored lessons are reached through the Storage Module
(\mref{mStorage}).

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{load} is called once, before any other access program, when
\progname{} starts.
\item The team checks a lesson before it is stored. The checks in
\texttt{load} only stop a damaged or incomplete lesson from being offered.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

The full semantics are given in Revision~0. In brief: \texttt{load} reads the
stored lessons into $\mathit{catalogue}$ and raises \texttt{badLesson} if a
lesson has no figure, an empty part or a difficulty whose index is not in its
figure; \texttt{lessons} gives the identifiers in $\mathit{catalogue}$ in order;
\texttt{name}, \texttt{category} and \texttt{figure} give those fields of one
lesson; \texttt{demonstration} gives the parts of the figure in the order the
example draws them (FR-LRN-003); \texttt{guide} gives the whole figure as the
outline at stage $T$, only the endpoints at stage $E$, and nothing at stage $I$
(FR-LRN-004 to FR-LRN-006); \texttt{difficulties} gives the predefined
difficulties of that stage of the lesson. Each raises \texttt{notLoaded} before
\texttt{load} and \texttt{unknownLesson} for an identifier not in
$\mathit{catalogue}$.

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
