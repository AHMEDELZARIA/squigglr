\section{MIS of the Attempt Module (\mref{mAttempt})}
\label{sec:mAttempt}

\subsection{Template Module}

Attempt

\subsection{Uses}

Input Device Module (\mref{mInputDevice}), Attempt Evaluation Module
(\mref{mEvaluation}), Stroke Module (\mref{mStroke}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

\begin{itemize}
\item AttemptT $= ?$, one attempt at one drawing stage of a lesson.
\item OutcomeT $= \{\mathit{open}, \mathit{passed}, \mathit{notPassed},
\mathit{invalid}, \mathit{interrupted}\}$, how the attempt ended, or
$\mathit{open}$ while it has not ended.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
new AttemptT & $\ell$: LessonIdT, $l$: DrawingStageT, $f$: FigureT,
$\mathit{ds}$: set of DifficultyT, $\mathit{stagePass}$: $\mathbb{R}$,
$\mathit{attemptIdle}$: $\mathbb{R}$ & AttemptT & -- \\
addStroke & $s$: StrokeT & -- & attemptEnded \\
checkIdle & -- & $\mathbb{B}$ & -- \\
hints & -- & $\mathbb{N}_0$ & -- \\
interrupt & -- & -- & attemptEnded \\
outcome & -- & OutcomeT & -- \\
evaluation & -- & EvaluationT & notEvaluated \\
strokes & -- & seq of StrokeT & -- \\
observed & -- & set of ObservedT & -- \\
lesson & -- & LessonIdT & -- \\
stage & -- & DrawingStageT & -- \\
endedAt & -- & DateTimeT & attemptOpen \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\ell$: LessonIdT and $l$: DrawingStageT, the lesson and stage of the
attempt.
\item $f$: FigureT and $\mathit{ds}$: set of DifficultyT, the lesson's figure
and the predefined difficulties of the stage.
\item $\mathit{stagePass}$: $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathit{attemptIdle}$:
$\mathbb{R}$, the learner's \texttt{STAGE\_PASS} and \texttt{ATTEMPT\_IDLE}
when the attempt started.
\item $X$: seq of StrokeT, the strokes drawn so far, in order.
\item $\mathit{last}$: EvaluationT, the evaluation after the most recent
stylus lift.
\item $\mathit{seen}$: set of ObservedT, every difficulty seen so far.
\item $h$: $\mathbb{N}_0$, the hints given so far, initially 0, and
$t_h$: DateTimeT and $n_h$: $\mathbb{N}_0$, when the last hint was given and
how many strokes $X$ had then.
\item $\mathit{out}$: OutcomeT.
\item $t_{\mathit{end}}$: DateTimeT, when the attempt ended.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{clock}$: DateTimeT, the current time.
\end{itemize}
The time of the child's last input is read from the Input Device Module
(\mref{mInputDevice}).

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item The Guided Lesson Control Module (\mref{mLessonControl}) creates an
attempt with values from the Lesson Catalogue (\mref{mCatalogue}) and the
Learner Settings Module (\mref{mSettings}), passes each finished stroke to
\texttt{addStroke} at the stylus lift that ends it, and calls
\texttt{checkIdle} often enough that the attempt ends within a second of
\texttt{ATTEMPT\_IDLE}.
\item The input device has already dropped duplicate reports of the same input
event and points it cannot map to the drawing area, and keeps strokes the child
draws separately, even where they overlap (SAF-AHM-001, SAF-AHM-002).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

The full semantics are given in Revision~0. In brief:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{new AttemptT} starts an open attempt with no strokes.
\item \texttt{addStroke} appends $s$ to $X$, evaluates all of $X$ with the
Attempt Evaluation Module (FR-LRN-008, FR-LRN-038), and updates
$\mathit{seen}$, marking a difficulty corrected when it is no longer observed.
A difficulty about something the child has drawn, such as a part drawn off the
figure, is noted at every stylus lift. A missed part or missed endpoint is
noted only from the evaluation with which the attempt ends, because at an early
stylus lift the child may simply not have drawn it yet (FR-REC-003). If the
evaluation passes, the attempt ends as $\mathit{passed}$ (FR-LRN-039).
\item \texttt{checkIdle} acts on an open attempt that has at least one stroke
when \texttt{ATTEMPT\_IDLE} has passed since the later of the last input and
$t_h$. If the last evaluation is invalid, the attempt ends as
$\mathit{invalid}$ (FR-LRN-010). Otherwise, if a required part or endpoint is
missing, $h < \texttt{MAX\_HINTS}$, and $h = 0$ or $|X| > n_h$, it gives a
hint: $h$ goes up by one, $t_h := \mathit{clock}$ and $n_h := |X|$, the
missing parts and endpoints are added to $\mathit{seen}$, the strokes stay, and
the output is true (FR-LRN-024). A missing part the child then draws is marked
corrected (FR-REC-003). In every other case the attempt ends as
$\mathit{notPassed}$. The output is false unless a hint was given. An attempt
with no strokes is left open.
\item \texttt{interrupt} ends the attempt as $\mathit{interrupted}$, keeping
its strokes and difficulties and no evaluation result (FR-LRN-009,
FR-REC-005).
\item When the attempt ends, $t_{\mathit{end}}$ is set from
$\mathit{clock}$.
\item \texttt{hints} gives $h$. The other programs give the state. \texttt{evaluation} raises
\texttt{notEvaluated} before the first stroke and after the attempt ends as
$\mathit{interrupted}$, and \texttt{endedAt} raises \texttt{attemptOpen}
while the attempt is open. \texttt{addStroke} and \texttt{interrupt} raise
\texttt{attemptEnded} when the attempt has already ended.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
