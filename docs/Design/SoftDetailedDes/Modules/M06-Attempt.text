\section{MIS of the Attempt Module (\mref{mAttempt})}
\label{sec:mAttempt}

\subsection{Template Module}

Attempt

\subsection{Uses}

Input Device Module (\mref{mInputDevice}), Attempt Evaluation Module
(\mref{mEvaluation}), Stroke Module (\mref{mStroke}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

\begin{itemize}
\item AttemptT $= ?$, one attempt at one drawing stage of a lesson.
\item OutcomeT $= \{\mathit{open}, \mathit{passed}, \mathit{notPassed},
\mathit{invalid}, \mathit{interrupted}\}$, how the attempt ended, or
$\mathit{open}$ while it has not ended.
\item ObservedT = tuple of $(\mathit{difficulty}: \text{DifficultyT},
\mathit{corrected}: \mathbb{B})$, a predefined difficulty seen during the
attempt and whether the child corrected it later in the same attempt
(FR-REC-003).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
new AttemptT & $\ell$: LessonIdT, $l$: DrawingStageT, $f$: FigureT,
$\mathit{ds}$: set of DifficultyT, $\mathit{stagePass}$: $\mathbb{R}$,
$\mathit{attemptIdle}$: $\mathbb{R}$ & AttemptT & -- \\
addStroke & $s$: StrokeT & -- & attemptEnded \\
checkIdle & -- & -- & -- \\
interrupt & -- & -- & attemptEnded \\
outcome & -- & OutcomeT & -- \\
evaluation & -- & EvaluationT & notEvaluated \\
strokes & -- & seq of StrokeT & -- \\
observed & -- & set of ObservedT & -- \\
lesson & -- & LessonIdT & -- \\
stage & -- & DrawingStageT & -- \\
endedAt & -- & DateTimeT & attemptOpen \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\ell$: LessonIdT and $l$: DrawingStageT, the lesson and stage of the
attempt.
\item $f$: FigureT and $\mathit{ds}$: set of DifficultyT, the lesson's figure
and the predefined difficulties of the stage.
\item $\mathit{stagePass}$: $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathit{attemptIdle}$:
$\mathbb{R}$, the learner's \texttt{STAGE\_PASS} and \texttt{ATTEMPT\_IDLE}
when the attempt started.
\item $X$: seq of StrokeT, the strokes drawn so far, in order.
\item $\mathit{last}$: EvaluationT, the evaluation after the most recent
stylus lift.
\item $\mathit{seen}$: set of ObservedT, every difficulty seen so far.
\item $\mathit{out}$: OutcomeT.
\item $t_{\mathit{end}}$: DateTimeT, when the attempt ended.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

None. The time of the child's last input is read from the Input Device Module
(\mref{mInputDevice}).

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item The Guided Lesson Control Module (\mref{mLessonControl}) creates an
attempt with values from the Lesson Catalogue (\mref{mCatalogue}) and the
Learner Settings Module (\mref{mSettings}), passes each finished stroke to
\texttt{addStroke} at the stylus lift that ends it, and calls
\texttt{checkIdle} often enough that the attempt ends within a second of
\texttt{ATTEMPT\_IDLE}.
\item The input device has already dropped duplicate points and points it
cannot map to the drawing area (SAF-AHM-001, SAF-AHM-002).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

The full semantics are given in Revision~0. In brief:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{new AttemptT} starts an open attempt with no strokes.
\item \texttt{addStroke} appends $s$ to $X$, evaluates all of $X$ with the
Attempt Evaluation Module (FR-LRN-008), and updates $\mathit{seen}$, marking a
difficulty corrected when it is no longer observed. If the evaluation passes,
the attempt ends as $\mathit{passed}$ (FR-LRN-024).
\item \texttt{checkIdle} ends an open attempt that has at least one stroke when
\texttt{ATTEMPT\_IDLE} has passed since the last input: as $\mathit{invalid}$
if the last evaluation is invalid (FR-LRN-010), and otherwise as
$\mathit{notPassed}$. An attempt with no strokes is left open.
\item \texttt{interrupt} ends the attempt as $\mathit{interrupted}$, keeping
its strokes and difficulties and no evaluation result (FR-LRN-009,
FR-REC-005).
\item The other programs give the state. \texttt{evaluation} raises
\texttt{notEvaluated} before the first stroke, and \texttt{endedAt} raises
\texttt{attemptOpen} while the attempt is open.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
