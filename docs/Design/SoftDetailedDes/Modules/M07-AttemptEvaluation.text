\section{MIS of the Attempt Evaluation Module (\mref{mEvaluation})}
\label{sec:mEvaluation}

\subsection{Module}

AttemptEvaluation

\subsection{Uses}

Stroke Module (\mref{mStroke}), Geometry Module (\mref{mGeometry}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\texttt{TRACE\_TOL} = 5$, in percent of the figure's size (the diagonal
of its bounding box). This is the value proposed in the SRS; the team confirms
it in PH-1, before the first evaluation test.
\item $\texttt{PART\_COVER} = 90$, in percent. Proposed in the SRS and
confirmed with \texttt{TRACE\_TOL}.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
evaluate & $X$: seq of StrokeT, $f$: FigureT, $l$: DrawingStageT,
$\mathit{ds}$: set of DifficultyT, $\mathit{stagePass}$: $\mathbb{R}$ &
EvaluationT & emptyFigure \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

None.

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

None.

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{stagePass}$ is in the range of \texttt{STAGE\_PASS}, 70 to 90,
because the Learner Settings Module (\mref{mSettings}) does not save a value
outside it.
\item $\mathit{ds}$ holds only difficulties whose index is a part or endpoint
of $f$, because the Lesson Catalogue (\mref{mCatalogue}) does not offer a
lesson that breaks this.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

\noindent \texttt{evaluate}($X, f, l, \mathit{ds}, \mathit{stagePass}$): the
full semantics are given in Revision~0, following SRS Section~9.2. In brief:
\begin{itemize}
\item output: $\mathit{out} := (C, G(l), v, C = \mathit{complete} \land v \geq
\mathit{stagePass}, \mathit{obs})$, where $C$ is the class of $X$ against $f$
($\mathit{invalid}$ if $X$ cannot be interpreted, $\mathit{complete}$ if every
required part has at least \texttt{PART\_COVER} of its length within
\texttt{TRACE\_TOL} of a stroke and every required endpoint has a drawn point
within \texttt{TRACE\_TOL} of it, and $\mathit{incomplete}$ otherwise), $G(l)$
is $\mathit{assisted}$ at $T$ and $E$ and $\mathit{independent}$ at $I$
(FR-LRN-012), $v$ is the stage result $100 \cdot \min(\mathit{drawn},
\mathit{onFigure})$, and $\mathit{obs}$ is the set of difficulties in
$\mathit{ds}$ that the strokes show (FR-LRN-014). At stage $I$, the strokes are
first fitted to the figure's bounding box with \texttt{fitToBox} of the Geometry
Module (\mref{mGeometry}): moved and uniformly scaled, as in SRS Section~9.2.
The result is the same
every time for the same inputs (NFR-PERF-004) and matches the definition in SRS
Section~9.2 (NFR-PERF-010).
\item exception: $\mathit{exc} := (f.\mathit{parts} = \langle\rangle
\Rightarrow \text{emptyFigure})$.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

These are defined in Revision~0, from SRS Section~9.2:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{valid}$: seq of StrokeT $\rightarrow \mathbb{B}$
\item $\mathit{complete}$: seq of PathT $\times$ FigureT $\rightarrow
\mathbb{B}$
\item $\mathit{drawn}$: seq of PathT $\times$ FigureT $\rightarrow \mathbb{R}$,
the share of the figure's length within \texttt{TRACE\_TOL} of a stroke
\item $\mathit{onFigure}$: seq of PathT $\times$ FigureT $\rightarrow
\mathbb{R}$, the share of the drawn length within \texttt{TRACE\_TOL} of the
figure
\item $\mathit{tol}$: FigureT $\rightarrow \mathbb{R}$, \texttt{TRACE\_TOL} as
a distance on the drawing area for this figure
\end{itemize}
