\section{MIS of the Attempt Record Module (\mref{mRecord})}
\label{sec:mRecord}

\subsection{Module}

AttemptRecord

\subsection{Uses}

Stroke Module (\mref{mStroke}), Storage Module (\mref{mStorage}), Connection
Recovery Module (\mref{mRecovery}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

{\sloppy
\begin{itemize}
\item LessonRecordIdT, which identifies one lesson record.
\item RecordOutcomeT $= \{\mathit{passed}, \mathit{notPassed},
\mathit{interrupted}\}$.
\item EndingT $= \{\mathit{inProgress},$ $\mathit{completed},$
$\mathit{endedAtLimit},$ $\mathit{endedAtMax},$ $\mathit{endedIdle},$
$\mathit{unfinished}\}$, how a lesson ended (the lesson ending of the SRS data
dictionary, with SAF-RAY-009's ending for inactivity).
\item ResponseT $= \{\mathit{pending}, \mathit{accepted}, \mathit{declined},
\mathit{ignored}\}$, the adult's response to a go-back suggestion.
\item SaveT $= \{\mathit{saved}, \mathit{pending}\}$, whether the server has
confirmed a save, or the record is kept on the device until it does.
\item AttemptRecordT = tuple of $(\mathit{lesson}: \text{LessonIdT},$
$\mathit{stage}: \text{DrawingStageT},$ $\mathit{outcome}:
\text{RecordOutcomeT},$ $\mathit{result}: \mathbb{R},$ $\mathit{difficulties}: \text{set of ObservedT},$ $\mathit{settings}: \text{SettingsT},$
$\mathit{strokes}: \text{seq of StrokeT},$ $\mathit{endedAt}:
\text{DateTimeT})$, one attempt (FR-REC-001 to FR-REC-005). An interrupted
attempt has no result, so its $\mathit{result}$ is not used.
\item SuggestionT = tuple of $(\mathit{from}: \text{DrawingStageT},$
$\mathit{offeredAt}: \text{DateTimeT},$ $\mathit{response}: \text{ResponseT},$
$\mathit{stage}: \text{DrawingStageT} \cup \{\bot\})$, where
$\mathit{from}$ is the stage at which the failed attempts were made and
$\mathit{stage}$ is the earlier stage the adult chose on acceptance, or $\bot$
if the suggestion was not accepted (FR-REC-010).
\item LessonRecordT = tuple of $(\mathit{id}: \text{LessonRecordIdT},$
$\mathit{learner}: \text{LearnerIdT},$ $\mathit{lesson}: \text{LessonIdT},$
$\mathit{start}: \text{StageT},$ $\mathit{furthest}: \text{StageT},$
$\mathit{passes}: \text{DrawingStageT} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}_0,$
$\mathit{extensions}: \mathbb{N}_0,$ $\mathit{ending}: \text{EndingT},$
$\mathit{suggestions}: \text{seq of SuggestionT},$ $\mathit{startedAt}:
\text{DateTimeT})$, one start of a lesson (FR-REC-006, FR-REC-007,
FR-REC-010).
\end{itemize}
}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
startLesson & $a$: AdultIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT, $\ell$: LessonIdT, $l$: StageT &
LessonRecord\-IdT & notOwner \\
saveAttempt & $a$: AdultIdT, $r$: LessonRecordIdT, $x$: AttemptRecordT & SaveT
& unknownRecord, lessonEnded \\
recordExtension & $a$: AdultIdT, $r$: LessonRecordIdT & SaveT & unknownRecord,
lessonEnded \\
offerSuggestion & $a$: AdultIdT, $r$: LessonRecordIdT, $l$: DrawingStageT &
$\mathbb{N}_0$ & unknownRecord, lessonEnded \\
answerSuggestion & $a$: AdultIdT, $r$: LessonRecordIdT, $i$: $\mathbb{N}_0$,
$\mathit{resp}$: ResponseT, $l$: DrawingStageT $\cup$ $\{\bot\}$ & SaveT &
unknownRecord, unknownSuggestion, alreadyAnswered, lessonEnded \\
endLesson & $a$: AdultIdT, $r$: LessonRecordIdT, $e$: EndingT, $f$: StageT &
SaveT & unknownRecord, lessonEnded \\
lessonRecords & $a$: AdultIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT & seq of LessonRecordT &
notOwner, unavailable \\
attempts & $a$: AdultIdT, $r$: LessonRecordIdT & seq of AttemptRecordT &
unknownRecord, unavailable \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

None. The saved records are kept by the Storage Module (\mref{mStorage}), and
the records the server has not yet confirmed are kept on the device by the
Connection Recovery Module (\mref{mRecovery}).

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

None.

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item The Guided Lesson Control Module (\mref{mLessonControl}) is the only
module that writes records, and the Progress Summary Module (\mref{mSummary})
is the only module that reads them. The Adult View Module (\mref{mAdultView})
gets records from the Progress Summary, and gets the records not yet saved from
the Connection Recovery Module.
\item Who may delete records is still an open issue in the SRS (OI-02), so
deletion is left to the Storage Module and is not part of this interface yet.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

The full semantics are given in Revision~0. In brief:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{startLesson} starts a new lesson record for learner $p$, with
ending $\mathit{inProgress}$, even when the same lesson was started before, so
earlier attempts are kept (SAF-RAY-006). It raises \texttt{notOwner} if $p$ is
not one of $a$'s learners.
\item \texttt{saveAttempt} saves $x$ under the lesson record $r$, and so to the
learner of $r$ only (SAF-RAY-007, FR-REC-008). Each attempt is saved exactly
once, even when the Connection Recovery Module sends it again after the
connection is lost. The output is $\mathit{pending}$ while the record is kept on
the device (SAF-RAY-003).
\item \texttt{recordExtension}, \texttt{offerSuggestion} and
\texttt{answerSuggestion} add an extension, a suggestion and the adult's
response to the lesson record (SAF-RAY-006, FR-REC-010).
\texttt{offerSuggestion} gives the index of the new suggestion, offered at
stage $l$. \texttt{answerSuggestion} keeps $l$ as the chosen stage when
$\mathit{resp} = \mathit{accepted}$, and $\bot$ otherwise.
\item \texttt{endLesson} sets the ending and the furthest stage. After it, the
lesson record and its attempts never change (FR-REC-009), so every program that
writes to the record raises \texttt{lessonEnded}.
\item \texttt{lessonRecords} and \texttt{attempts} read back a learner's
records, oldest first.
\end{itemize}
Records hold only guided practice, never a creative drawing (FR-CRT-028), keep each
attempt's stage so that attempts with and without a guide stay apart
(SAF-MOU-002), and hold no label or judgement of the child (SAF-RAY-004).

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
