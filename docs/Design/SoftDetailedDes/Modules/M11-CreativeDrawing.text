\section{MIS of the Creative Drawing Module (\mref{mCreative})}
\label{sec:mCreative}

\subsection{Module}

CreativeDrawing

\subsection{Uses}

Input Device Module (\mref{mInputDevice}), Display Module (\mref{mDisplay}),
Stroke Module (\mref{mStroke}), Edit History Module (\mref{mHistory}), Storage
Module (\mref{mStorage}), Connection Recovery Module (\mref{mRecovery}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

{\sloppy
\begin{itemize}
\item StrokeIdT, which identifies one stroke in a drawing.
\item DrawingStrokeT = tuple of $(\mathit{id}: \text{StrokeIdT},$
$\mathit{path}: \text{PathT}, \mathit{colour}: \text{ColourIdT},$
$\mathit{width}: \mathbb{R})$, one editable stroke in a creative drawing.
\item DrawingT = tuple of $(\mathit{strokes}: \text{seq of DrawingStrokeT},$
$\mathit{refined}: \mathbb{B}, \mathit{version}: \mathbb{N}_0)$, the current
editable drawing, whether it holds an unrestored refinement, and its change
version.
\item PartT $= \text{set of StrokeIdT}$, the selected strokes.
\end{itemize}
}

DrawingIdT is defined by the Refinement Control Module
(\mref{mRefineControl}), which identifies saved creative drawings.

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
newDrawing & $a$: AdultIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT & DrawingIdT & notOwner,
unavailable \\
drawings & $a$: AdultIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT & seq of DrawingIdT & notOwner,
unavailable \\
open & $a$: AdultIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT, $d$: DrawingIdT & DrawingT &
notOwner, unknownDrawing, unavailable \\
current & $d$: DrawingIdT & DrawingT & unknownDrawing \\
processInput & -- & DrawingT & noDrawing \\
setStyle & $c$: ColourIdT, $w$: $\mathbb{R}$ & -- & noDrawing,
invalidWidth \\
select & $I$: PartT & -- & noDrawing, unknownStroke \\
move & $\Delta$: PointT & DrawingT & noSelection \\
resize & $r$: $\mathbb{R}$ & DrawingT & noSelection, nonPositiveScale \\
recolour & $c$: ColourIdT & DrawingT & noSelection \\
erase & -- & DrawingT & noSelection \\
undo & -- & DrawingT & noUndo \\
redo & -- & DrawingT & noRedo \\
replacePaths & $d$: DrawingIdT, $P$: seq of PathT, $v$: $\mathbb{N}_0$,
$\mathit{refined}$: $\mathbb{B}$ & DrawingT & unknownDrawing, outOfDate,
wrongStrokeCount \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{active}$: DrawingIdT $\cup$ $\{\bot\}$, the open drawing.
\item $X$: seq of DrawingStrokeT, the strokes of the open drawing in order.
\item $I$: PartT, the selected strokes.
\item $v$: $\mathbb{N}_0$, the version of the open drawing.
\item $\mathit{dirty}$: $\mathbb{B}$, whether the latest change has not yet
been confirmed saved.
\item $s$: StrokeT $\cup$ $\{\bot\}$, the contact currently being collected,
or $\bot$ when no stylus contact is open.
\item $c$: ColourIdT and $w$: $\mathbb{R}$, the style used for new strokes.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

None. Saved drawings are reached through the Storage Module
(\mref{mStorage}); changes awaiting a successful save are kept by the
Connection Recovery Module (\mref{mRecovery}).

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

{\sloppy
\begin{itemize}
\item $a$ is the signed in adult that the Adult Access Module (\mref{mAccess}) 
gives.
\item $p$ is the active learner.
\item \texttt{processInput} drains the Input Device Module's
(\mref{mInputDevice}) accepted events once, and the caller sets the colour and
width for new strokes with \texttt{setStyle}.
\item Refinement is applied only through the Refinement Control Module
(\mref{mRefineControl}); \texttt{replacePaths} is used only after that module
has checked the result or is restoring saved original paths.
\end{itemize}
}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

Drawings are saved and reopened for the active learner while remaining 
editable and are not counted as attempts (FR-CRT-023, FR-CRT-024, FR-CRT-028). 
\texttt{processInput} builds a StrokeT from each down/move/up contact using 
the Stroke Module (\mref{mStroke}) and draws it locally as it arrives. Each 
stroke or edit action is counted as one change.
The Edit History Module (\mref{mHistory}) keeps them undoable. Selection and
editing affect only the selected strokes; move applies an offset and resize
scales about the centre of the selected part's bounding box. A refinement 
replacement only changes the paths of the strokes but keeps stroke order, colour, and 
width. The drawing's version is incremented for each change and refinement
replacements with stale versions are rejected.

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
