\section{MIS of the Child Screen Module (\mref{mChildScreen})}
\label{sec:mChildScreen}

\subsection{Module}

ChildScreen

\subsection{Uses}

Guided Lesson Control Module (\mref{mLessonControl}), Creative Drawing Module
(\mref{mCreative}), Refinement Control Module (\mref{mRefineControl}), Learner
Profile Module (\mref{mProfile}), Learner Settings Module (\mref{mSettings}),
Display Module (\mref{mDisplay}), Audio Output Module (\mref{mAudio}), Feedback
Module (\mref{mFeedback}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

\begin{itemize}
\item ScreenT $= \{\mathit{lesson}, \mathit{creative}\}$, the child-facing
screens.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
showLesson & -- & -- & noLesson \\
addStroke & $s$: StrokeT & ReportT & noAttempt \\
tick & -- & ReportT & noLesson \\
showCreative & -- & -- & noActiveLearner, unavailable \\
creativeInput & -- & DrawingT & noDrawing \\
selectPart & $I$: PartT & -- & noDrawing, unknownStroke \\
movePart & $\Delta$: PointT & -- & noSelection \\
resizePart & $r$: $\mathbb{R}$ & -- & noSelection, nonPositiveScale \\
recolourPart & $c$: ColourIdT & -- & noSelection \\
erasePart & -- & -- & noSelection \\
undo & -- & -- & noUndo \\
redo & -- & -- & noRedo \\
finishDrawing & -- & -- & noDrawing \\
restoreDrawing & -- & -- & noDrawing, noOriginal \\
setRefinement & $\mathit{on}$: $\mathbb{B}$ & -- & noActiveLearner,
unavailable \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{screen}$: ScreenT $\cup$ $\{\bot\}$, the child-facing screen
shown, or $\bot$ before one is opened.
\item $\mathit{avatar}$: AvatarT, the active learner's avatar.
\item $\mathit{drawing}$: DrawingIdT $\cup$ $\{\bot\}$, the creative drawing
shown, or $\bot$ when the creative space is closed.
\item $\mathit{report}$: ReportT $\cup$ $\{\bot\}$, the latest guided-lesson
report, or $\bot$ when none is showing.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{screenPixels}$: the pixels of the browser window.
\item $\mathit{clock}$: DateTimeT, the current time used to tick a guided
lesson.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{tick} is called once a second.
\item While a guided lesson is active, \texttt{showLesson} is called and the
screen passes each finished stroke to the Guided Lesson Control Module
(\mref{mLessonControl}) every \texttt{tick}.
\item \texttt{showCreative} is called with an active learner. Drawing edits
are sent to the Creative Drawing Module (\mref{mCreative}); finishing and
restoring are sent to the Refinement Control Module (\mref{mRefineControl}).
\texttt{creativeInput} is called while the creative space is open to
show new stylus input.
\item Every feedback report is shown with the Feedback Module
(\mref{mFeedback}), rendered by the Display Module (\mref{mDisplay}), and its
sound is played by the Audio Output Module (\mref{mAudio}).
\item Child-facing controls use images and icons instead of text and have
touch targets at least \texttt{TARGET\_SIZE\_MIN} (NFR-UX-002, NFR-UX-018).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

\texttt{showLesson} shows the active guided stage, its demonstration or guide, 
the learner's strokes and avatar; \texttt{addStroke} and \texttt{tick} relay 
lesson input and time checks and show the resulting feedback. 
\texttt{showCreative} opens the active learner's editable drawing; 
\texttt{creativeInput} relays new input to it. 
The edit programs relay the child's
selection and changes; \texttt{finishDrawing} asks for automatic refinement
using the learner's current setting, and \texttt{restoreDrawing} restores the
original. \texttt{setRefinement} saves the child's refinement choice for the
active learner.

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
