\section{MIS of the Adult Access Module (\mref{mAccess})}
\label{sec:mAccess}

\subsection{Module}

AdultAccess

\subsection{Uses}

Audit Log Module (\mref{mLog}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None. \texttt{IDLE\_TIMEOUT} and the form of the adult check are this module's
secret.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

{\sloppy
\begin{itemize}
\item CredentialsT = tuple of $(\mathit{email}: \text{string},$
$\mathit{password}: \text{string})$, what an adult enters to sign up or sign
in.
\item SignUpResultT $= \{\mathit{created}, \mathit{emailInUse},
\mathit{notAccepted}\}$, where $\mathit{notAccepted}$ means the credentials do
not meet the rules of the sign-in service.
\item AdultActionT $= \{\mathit{lessonChoice},$ $\mathit{suggestionAnswer},$
$\mathit{learnerSwitch},$ $\mathit{settingsChange},$
$\mathit{drawingRestore}\}$, the actions a child must not take alone: choosing
a lesson and its starting stage and answering a go-back suggestion (SAF-RAY-001,
FR-LRN-018), switching the active learner (SAF-RAY-001), changing a learner's
settings (SAF-RAY-008) and restoring a drawing (FR-CRT-019).
\end{itemize}
}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
init & -- & -- & -- \\
signUp & $c$: CredentialsT & SignUpResultT & unavailable \\
signIn & $c$: CredentialsT & $\mathbb{B}$ & unavailable \\
signOut & -- & -- & notSignedIn \\
isSignedIn & -- & $\mathbb{B}$ & -- \\
adult & -- & AdultIdT & notSignedIn \\
adultCheck & $\mathit{act}$: AdultActionT & $\mathbb{B}$ & notSignedIn \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{lastInput}$: DateTimeT, the time of the last input to the page.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{auth}$: tuple of $(\mathit{accounts}: \text{set of tuple of }
(\mathit{id}: \text{AdultIdT}, \mathit{c}: \text{CredentialsT}),$
$\mathit{session}: \text{AdultIdT} \cup \{\bot\})$, the accounts of the hosted
sign-in service and the session of this browser (NFR-OPS-002).
\item $\mathit{screen}$: seq of seq of PixelT, the pixels of the browser window,
where PixelT is the colour of one pixel. This module shows only the adult check
on it.
\item $\mathit{input}$: seq of InputEventT, the key presses and pointer events
on the page, each with its time. It is used to time inactivity (SAF-SHA-006)
and to read the answer to the adult check.
\item $\mathit{clock}$: DateTimeT, the current time.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{init} is called once, when the page is opened, before any other
access program.
\item A wrong password or an email already in use is the adult's mistake, not
the caller's, so it is an output (false, or $\mathit{emailInUse}$), not an
exception. Callers use \texttt{isSignedIn} before the programs that raise
\texttt{notSignedIn}.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

The full semantics are given in Revision~0. In brief:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{init} sets $\mathit{lastInput}$ to the current time. A session
kept by the browser from an earlier visit stays open.
\item \texttt{signUp} creates an account with the sign-in service and signs the
adult in. It collects nothing but the email and password (NFR-SEC-004).
\item \texttt{signIn} opens a session and gives true, or gives false and logs a
failed sign-in with the Audit Log Module (NFR-SEC-005). \texttt{signOut} ends
the session, so it cannot be used again (NFR-SEC-002).
\item \texttt{isSignedIn} is true while a session is open. A session with no
input for \texttt{IDLE\_TIMEOUT} is ended and the timeout is logged
(SAF-SHA-006, NFR-SEC-005).
\item \texttt{adult} gives the signed-in adult. Every module that reads or saves
an adult's data is given this value by its caller (SAF-SHA-001).
\item \texttt{adultCheck} shows a short check that a young child cannot pass
alone and gives whether it was passed. When it gives false, the caller does
not carry out $\mathit{act}$ (SAF-RAY-001, SAF-RAY-008).
\end{itemize}
\texttt{unavailable} is raised when the sign-in service does not answer.

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
