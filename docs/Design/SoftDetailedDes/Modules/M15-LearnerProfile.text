\section{MIS of the Learner Profile Module (\mref{mProfile})}
\label{sec:mProfile}

\subsection{Module}

LearnerProfile

\subsection{Uses}

Storage Module (\mref{mStorage}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

\begin{itemize}
\item AvatarT, which identifies one of the avatar pictures that \progname{}
offers. A profile never holds a photo or the name of the child (BR-001,
NFR-SEC-004).
\item LearnerT = tuple of $(\mathit{id}: \text{LearnerIdT},$
$\mathit{avatar}: \text{AvatarT})$, one learner profile (the learner profile of
the SRS data dictionary).
\item ActiveT = tuple of $(\mathit{adult}: \text{AdultIdT},$
$\mathit{learner}: \text{LearnerT})$, the active learner on this device and the
adult who chose it.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
init & -- & -- & -- \\
avatars & -- & seq of AvatarT & -- \\
create & $a$: AdultIdT, $\mathit{av}$: AvatarT & LearnerIdT & unavailable \\
learners & $a$: AdultIdT & seq of LearnerT & unavailable \\
setActive & $a$: AdultIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT & -- & notOwner, unavailable \\
hasActive & -- & $\mathbb{B}$ & -- \\
active & -- & ActiveT & noActiveLearner \\
clearActive & -- & -- & -- \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{current}$: ActiveT $\cup$ $\{\bot\}$, the active learner on this
device, or $\bot$ when there is none.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

None. The profiles are kept through the Storage Module (\mref{mStorage}).

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{init} is called once, when the page is opened, before any other
access program.
\item $a$ is the adult that the Adult Access Module (\mref{mAccess}) gives as
signed in.
\item Before \texttt{setActive}, the caller has had the adult pass the adult
check for $\mathit{learnerSwitch}$ (SAF-RAY-001). Only the Adult View Module
(\mref{mAdultView}) calls \texttt{create}, \texttt{setActive} and
\texttt{clearActive}.
\item Callers use \texttt{hasActive} before \texttt{active}.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

The full semantics are given in Revision~0. In brief:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{init} sets $\mathit{current}$ to $\bot$, so no learner is active
until an adult chooses one.
\item \texttt{avatars} gives the avatars an adult can choose from.
\item \texttt{create} saves a new profile with an identifier that \progname{}
generates, and gives that identifier (BR-001). It does not change
$\mathit{current}$.
\item \texttt{learners} gives $a$'s profiles in the order they were created, and
only those (NFR-SEC-001).
\item \texttt{setActive} makes $p$ the active learner, so that every attempt and
drawing after it is saved to $p$ only (SAF-RAY-007). It raises
\texttt{notOwner} if $p$ is not one of $a$'s learners.
\item \texttt{hasActive} tells whether there is an active learner,
\texttt{active} gives it, and \texttt{clearActive} sets $\mathit{current}$ to
$\bot$, as the Adult View does when the adult signs out.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
