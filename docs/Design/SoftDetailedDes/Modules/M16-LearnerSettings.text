\section{MIS of the Learner Settings Module (\mref{mSettings})}
\label{sec:mSettings}

\subsection{Module}

LearnerSettings

\subsection{Uses}

Storage Module (\mref{mStorage}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None. The range and default of each adult-set value are this module's secret;
\texttt{range} gives them. Their values are those of the symbolic constants of
the SRS (BR-008, BR-009).

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

{\sloppy
\begin{itemize}
\item SettingNameT $= \{\mathit{stagePass},$ $\mathit{difficultyStreak},$
$\mathit{attemptLimit},$ $\mathit{improveWindow},$
$\mathit{attemptExtension},$ $\mathit{attemptLimitMax},$
$\mathit{attemptIdle},$ $\mathit{lessonIdlePrompt},$
$\mathit{lessonIdleEnd}\}$, the names of the fields of SettingsT
(Table~\ref{tab:shared-types}).
\item RangeT = tuple of $(\mathit{min}: \mathbb{N}_0,$ $\mathit{max}:
\mathbb{N}_0,$ $\mathit{default}: \mathbb{N}_0)$, the team-set range of one
adult-set value and its default, in the unit of its SRS constant: points out of
100 for $\mathit{stagePass}$, seconds for the three idle times, and attempts for
the others.
\item OptionsT = tuple of $(\mathit{audio}: \mathbb{B},$ $\mathit{refinement}:
\mathbb{B})$, the audio setting (NFR-UX-010) and the refinement setting
(FR-CRT-030) of one learner. The SRS data dictionary also lists accessibility
settings, which the SRS has not defined yet, so they are not part of this
interface.
\end{itemize}
}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
settings & $a$: AdultIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT & SettingsT & notOwner,
unavailable \\
range & $n$: SettingNameT & RangeT & -- \\
check & $s$: SettingsT & set of SettingNameT & -- \\
save & $a$: AdultIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT, $s$: SettingsT & -- & invalidSettings,
notOwner, unavailable \\
options & $a$: AdultIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT & OptionsT & notOwner, unavailable \\
setAudio & $a$: AdultIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT, $\mathit{on}$: $\mathbb{B}$ & -- &
notOwner, unavailable \\
setRefinement & $a$: AdultIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT, $\mathit{on}$: $\mathbb{B}$ &
-- & notOwner, unavailable \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

None. The saved settings are kept through the Storage Module
(\mref{mStorage}).

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

None.

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item $a$ is the adult that the Adult Access Module (\mref{mAccess}) gives as
signed in.
\item Before \texttt{setAudio}, and before a \texttt{save} that changes values
already saved for $p$, the caller has had the adult pass the adult check for
$\mathit{settingsChange}$ (SAF-RAY-008). The first \texttt{save} for a new
profile needs no adult check, because it changes no saved value and the SRS
has no adult check when a profile is created (use case UC-001).
\texttt{setRefinement} needs no adult check, because the child may turn
refinement off and on in the creative space (FR-CRT-030).
\item The team-set ranges keep \texttt{LESSON\_IDLE\_END} below
\texttt{IDLE\_TIMEOUT} for every allowed value, so this module does not check
that rule.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

The full semantics are given in Revision~0. In brief:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{settings} gives $p$'s saved values, with the default for each
value the adult has not chosen (the middle of its range). \texttt{options}
gives $p$'s audio and refinement settings.
\item \texttt{range} gives the range and default of value $n$.
\item \texttt{check} gives the values of $s$ that are outside their range or
break a rule between values: \texttt{ATTEMPT\_LIMIT} $>$
\texttt{DIFFICULTY\_STREAK}, \texttt{ATTEMPT\_LIMIT\_MAX} $\geq$
\texttt{ATTEMPT\_LIMIT}, and \texttt{ATTEMPT\_IDLE} $<$
\texttt{LESSON\_IDLE\_PROMPT} $<$ \texttt{LESSON\_IDLE\_END}. Both values of a
broken rule are given. An empty set means $s$ can be saved.
\item \texttt{save} saves $s$ for $p$ and raises \texttt{invalidSettings} if
$\texttt{check}(s) \neq \emptyset$, so no value outside its range is ever saved
(SAF-RAY-008). The values a lesson uses are those read when it is chosen.
\item \texttt{setAudio} and \texttt{setRefinement} save one field of $p$'s
options.
\end{itemize}
Each program raises \texttt{notOwner} if $p$ is not one of $a$'s learners.

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
