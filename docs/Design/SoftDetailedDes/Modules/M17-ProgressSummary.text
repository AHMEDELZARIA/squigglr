\section{MIS of the Progress Summary Module (\mref{mSummary})}
\label{sec:mSummary}

\subsection{Module}

ProgressSummary

\subsection{Uses}

Attempt Record Module (\mref{mRecord}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{PRACTICE\_ONLY}: string $=$ ``This summary describes practice
only. It is not an assessment or a diagnosis.'', the practice-only statement
that the Adult View Module (\mref{mAdultView}) shows on every summary
(SAF-RAY-004).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

The types LessonRecordT, AttemptRecordT and EndingT are those of the Attempt
Record Module (\mref{mRecord}).

{\sloppy
\begin{itemize}
\item ViewChoiceT = tuple of $(\mathit{lessons}: \text{set of LessonIdT},$
$\mathit{from}: \text{DateTimeT} \cup \{\bot\},$ $\mathit{to}:
\text{DateTimeT} \cup \{\bot\})$, the lessons and dates an adult asks a summary
to cover (FR-PRG-007). The empty set covers every lesson, and $\bot$ sets no
limit on the dates. Which recorded values are shown is chosen in the Adult View
Module (\mref{mAdultView}).
\item StageStatusT $= \{\mathit{notAttempted}, \mathit{attempted}\}$
(FR-PRG-010).
\item StageSummaryT = tuple of $(\mathit{stage}: \text{DrawingStageT},$
$\mathit{status}: \text{StageStatusT},$ $\mathit{passed}: \text{seq of
AttemptRecordT},$ $\mathit{notPassed}: \text{seq of AttemptRecordT},$
$\mathit{interrupted}: \text{seq of AttemptRecordT})$, the attempts at one
drawing stage of one run of a lesson. Stages $T$ and $E$ are drawn with a guide
and stage $I$ without one (FR-PRG-003, FR-PRG-009).
\item RunT = tuple of $(\mathit{record}: \text{LessonRecordT},$
$\mathit{stages}: \text{seq of StageSummaryT})$, one start of a lesson, with its
starting stage, furthest stage, passes, extensions and ending (FR-PRG-002).
\item LessonSummaryT = tuple of $(\mathit{lesson}: \text{LessonIdT},$
$\mathit{runs}: \text{seq of RunT},$ $\mathit{limitEndings}: \mathbb{N}_0,$
$\mathit{extensions}: \mathbb{N}_0)$, every run of one lesson in the order they
started, with the number of runs that ended at a limit and the number of
extensions granted (SAF-RAY-006).
\item RecurringT = tuple of $(\mathit{lesson}: \text{LessonIdT},$
$\mathit{stage}: \text{DrawingStageT},$ $\mathit{difficulty}:
\text{DifficultyT},$ $\mathit{seen}: \text{seq of AttemptRecordT},$
$\mathit{corrected}: \text{seq of AttemptRecordT})$, one recurring difficulty
(FR-PRG-004).
\item SummaryT = tuple of $(\mathit{learner}: \text{LearnerIdT},$
$\mathit{lessons}: \text{seq of LessonSummaryT},$ $\mathit{recurring}:
\text{seq of RecurringT},$ $\mathit{latest}: \text{DateTimeT} \cup \{\bot\})$,
the progress summary of one learner (FR-PRG-001). It has no field that combines
results across stages or lessons (FR-PRG-012).
\end{itemize}
}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
summary & $a$: AdultIdT, $p$: LearnerIdT, $v$: ViewChoiceT & SummaryT &
notOwner, unavailable \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

None. A summary is built from the records each time it is asked for, and has
no data of its own.

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

None.

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item $a$ is the adult that the Adult Access Module (\mref{mAccess}) gives as
signed in.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

The full semantics are given in Revision~0. In brief, \texttt{summary} reads
$p$'s lesson records and their attempts with \texttt{lessonRecords} and
\texttt{attempts} of the Attempt Record Module, keeps those of the lessons and
dates in $v$, and builds the summary from them:
\begin{itemize}
\item Each lesson's runs are kept in the order they started, so no earlier
attempt is hidden by a later run (SAF-RAY-006). A run's limit ending is an
ending of $\mathit{endedAtLimit}$ or $\mathit{endedAtMax}$.
\item Each run has a stage summary for every drawing stage, so attempts with and
without a guide are never added together (FR-PRG-003, SAF-MOU-002), interrupted
attempts are kept apart from the others (FR-PRG-009), and a stage the child did
not reach is $\mathit{notAttempted}$ (FR-PRG-010).
\item A difficulty is recurring when it was seen in at least \texttt{RECUR\_MIN}
of the learner's attempts at the same stage of the same lesson.
$\mathit{corrected}$ holds the attempts in which the child corrected it
(FR-PRG-004). \texttt{RECUR\_MIN} is this module's secret, proposed at 3 in the
SRS.
\item $\mathit{latest}$ is the end time of the learner's latest attempt,
including one saved just before the summary was asked for (FR-PRG-011), or
$\bot$ if there is none. A learner with no records gets a summary with no
lessons (FR-PRG-008).
\item Every figure in the summary holds the attempt records behind it, so that
the adult can open them (FR-PRG-005). Each record keeps the adult-set values it
was made with (SAF-RAY-008).
\end{itemize}
\texttt{summary} raises \texttt{notOwner} if $p$ is not one of $a$'s learners,
and \texttt{unavailable} if the records cannot be read.

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
