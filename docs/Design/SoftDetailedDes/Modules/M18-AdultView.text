\section{MIS of the Adult View Module (\mref{mAdultView})}
\label{sec:mAdultView}

\subsection{Module}

AdultView

\subsection{Uses}

Display Module (\mref{mDisplay}), Guided Lesson Control Module
(\mref{mLessonControl}), Lesson Catalogue Module (\mref{mCatalogue}), Refinement
Control Module (\mref{mRefineControl}), Adult Access Module (\mref{mAccess}),
Learner Profile Module (\mref{mProfile}), Learner Settings Module
(\mref{mSettings}), Progress Summary Module (\mref{mSummary}), Connection
Recovery Module (\mref{mRecovery}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

The inputs below use CredentialsT of the Adult Access Module, AvatarT of the
Learner Profile Module, ViewChoiceT of the Progress Summary Module, and
AttemptRecordT of the Attempt Record Module (\mref{mRecord}), which this module
only gets from the Progress Summary Module and passes on. DrawingIdT identifies
one saved creative drawing and is the type of the Refinement Control Module.

{\sloppy
\begin{itemize}
\item ViewT $= \{\mathit{signIn},$ $\mathit{profiles},$ $\mathit{settings},$
$\mathit{lessonChoice},$ $\mathit{progress},$ $\mathit{attempts},$
$\mathit{drawings},$ $\mathit{unsaved}\}$, the adult views.
\item SummaryPartT $= \{\mathit{startingStage},$ $\mathit{furthestStage},$
$\mathit{passes},$ $\mathit{lessonEnding},$ $\mathit{limitCounts},$
$\mathit{recurring},$ $\mathit{interrupted},$ $\mathit{notAttempted}\}$, the
recorded values an adult can choose to show in a progress summary
(FR-PRG-007).
\end{itemize}
}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

There is one access program for each action an adult takes in the adult views.
The inputs are what the adult enters or selects in the view.

\begin{accessprograms}
init & -- & -- & -- \\
signUp & $c$: CredentialsT & -- & -- \\
signIn & $c$: CredentialsT & -- & -- \\
signOut & -- & -- & -- \\
addLearner & $\mathit{av}$: AvatarT, $s$: SettingsT & -- & -- \\
switchLearner & $p$: LearnerIdT & -- & -- \\
saveSettings & $p$: LearnerIdT, $s$: SettingsT & -- & -- \\
setAudio & $p$: LearnerIdT, $\mathit{on}$: $\mathbb{B}$ & -- & -- \\
chooseLesson & $\ell$: LessonIdT, $l$: StageT & -- & -- \\
acceptSuggestion & $l$: DrawingStageT & -- & -- \\
declineSuggestion & -- & -- & -- \\
showProgress & $p$: LearnerIdT, $v$: ViewChoiceT, $\mathit{ps}$: set of
SummaryPartT & -- & -- \\
showAttempts & $\mathit{rs}$: seq of AttemptRecordT & -- & -- \\
showDrawings & $p$: LearnerIdT & -- & -- \\
restoreDrawing & $d$: DrawingIdT & -- & -- \\
showUnsaved & -- & -- & -- \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{view}$: ViewT, the view shown.
\item $\mathit{parts}$: set of SummaryPartT, the recorded values shown in a
summary.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{screen}$: seq of seq of PixelT, the pixels of the browser window
while an adult view is shown, where PixelT is the colour of one pixel.
\item $\mathit{input}$: seq of InputEventT, the adult's key presses and pointer
events in the adult views, each with its time.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{init} is called each time an adult view is opened, before any
other access program.
\item \texttt{showAttempts} is given the attempt records behind a figure of the
summary on the screen, which the adult has just selected.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

The full semantics are given in Revision~0. In brief:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{init} sets $\mathit{view}$ to $\mathit{profiles}$ if an adult is
signed in and to $\mathit{signIn}$ if not, and sets $\mathit{parts}$ to every
recorded value.
\item Each program carries out one adult action and shows its result. A problem,
such as a wrong password, a setting out of range, an unsupported device
(SAF-RAY-002) or a server that does not answer, is shown on the screen in plain
words (NFR-UX-017), so no program raises an exception.
\item \texttt{switchLearner}, \texttt{saveSettings}, \texttt{setAudio} and
\texttt{restoreDrawing} first run the adult check of the Adult Access Module,
and change nothing if it is not passed (SAF-RAY-001, SAF-RAY-008, FR-CRT-019).
The lesson choice and the answers to a go-back suggestion are passed to
\texttt{select} and \texttt{answerSuggestion} of the Guided Lesson Control
Module, which run the check themselves.
\item \texttt{switchLearner} and \texttt{signOut} first call \texttt{stop} of
the Guided Lesson Control Module. \texttt{signOut} also clears the active
learner.
\item \texttt{addLearner} creates the profile and saves its settings, so a new
adult can set up a learner in one view (NFR-UX-009). \texttt{saveSettings}
shows the values that \texttt{check} of the Learner Settings Module gives,
and saves only when there are none.
\item The lesson choice view lists the lessons of the Lesson Catalogue Module
by name.
\item \texttt{showProgress} shows the summary of $p$ for $v$ with the values in
$\mathit{ps}$, and always with \texttt{PRACTICE\_ONLY} (SAF-RAY-004).
\texttt{showAttempts} shows every value of each record in $\mathit{rs}$, with
its strokes drawn by the Display Module (FR-PRG-005, FR-PRG-006).
\item \texttt{showDrawings} lists $p$'s saved drawings that can be restored.
\item \texttt{showUnsaved} lists the attempts and drawings that are still kept
on the device and not yet saved (SAF-RAY-003, SAF-SHA-003).
\item The adult views look different from the child's screens (NFR-UX-003),
every action can be done with the keyboard alone (NFR-UX-023), and their text
can be enlarged (NFR-UX-024).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
