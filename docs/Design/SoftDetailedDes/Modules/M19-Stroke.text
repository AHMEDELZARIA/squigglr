\section{MIS of the Stroke Module (\mref{mStroke})}
\label{sec:mStroke}

\subsection{Module}

Stroke

\subsection{Uses}

None.

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

\begin{itemize}
\item TimedPointT = tuple of $(\mathit{point}: \text{PointT},$
$\mathit{time}: \text{DateTimeT})$, one position reported during a stroke and
its time.
\end{itemize}

StrokeT is the abstract data type shared between modules in
Table~\ref{tab:shared-types}; its representation is hidden here.

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
new StrokeT & -- & StrokeT & -- \\
addPoint & $s$: StrokeT, $p$: PointT, $t$: DateTimeT & -- & strokeFinished \\
finish & $s$: StrokeT & -- & strokeFinished \\
points & $s$: StrokeT & seq of TimedPointT & -- \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

None at the module level. Each StrokeT holds its ordered timed points and
whether it is finished.

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

None.

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item Points are in CSS pixels on the drawing area and times are in UTC.
\item The Input Device Module (\mref{mInputDevice}) supplies points in contact
order, with duplicate reports removed.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

\texttt{newStroke} creates an empty stroke; \texttt{addPoint} appends a point 
and its time;
\texttt{finish} marks the contact complete; and \texttt{points} gives its
samples in order.
