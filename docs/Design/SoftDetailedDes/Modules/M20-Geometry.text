\section{MIS of the Geometry Module (\mref{mGeometry})}
\label{sec:mGeometry}

\subsection{Module}

Geometry

\subsection{Uses}

Stroke Module (\mref{mStroke}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

\begin{itemize}
\item BoxT = tuple of $(\mathit{minX}: \mathbb{R}, \mathit{minY}: \mathbb{R},
\mathit{maxX}: \mathbb{R}, \mathit{maxY}: \mathbb{R})$, the smallest rectangle,
with sides parallel to the drawing area, that holds a set of paths.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
toPath & $s$: StrokeT & PathT & -- \\
length & $P$: PathT & $\mathbb{R}$ & -- \\
distance & $p$: PointT, $Q$: seq of PathT & $\mathbb{R}$ & emptyPath \\
lengthWithin & $P$: PathT, $Q$: seq of PathT, $d$: $\mathbb{R}$ &
$\mathbb{R}$ & emptyPath, negativeDistance \\
boundingBox & $Q$: seq of PathT & BoxT & emptyPath \\
diagonal & $b$: BoxT & $\mathbb{R}$ & -- \\
fitToBox & $Q$: seq of PathT, $b$: BoxT & seq of PathT & emptyPath \\
smooth & $P$: PathT & PathT & -- \\
simplify & $P$: PathT, $d$: $\mathbb{R}$ & PathT & negativeDistance \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

None.

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

None.

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

None. Every distance is in the units of the drawing area, and no access program
depends on the drawing library, so the library can be replaced without changing
this interface.

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

The full semantics are given in Revision~0. In brief:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{toPath} gives the points of a stroke, in order, as a path.
\item \texttt{length} gives the length of a path, and 0 for a path with fewer
than two points.
\item \texttt{distance} gives the smallest distance from $p$ to any of the
paths in $Q$.
\item \texttt{lengthWithin} gives the length of the parts of $P$ that are
within distance $d$ of a path in $Q$. The Attempt Evaluation Module uses it for
how much of a required part is drawn and how much of a stroke lies on the
figure.
\item \texttt{boundingBox} and \texttt{diagonal} give the bounding box of $Q$
and the length of its diagonal, which sets the size of a figure for
\texttt{TRACE\_TOL}.
\item \texttt{fitToBox} moves and uniformly scales $Q$ so that its bounding box
is centred on $b$ and its longer side matches $b$'s (SRS Section~9.2, stage
$I$).
\item \texttt{smooth} and \texttt{simplify} give a smoother path and a path
with fewer points that stays within $d$ of $P$, for the Refinement Control
Module (\mref{mRefineControl}).
\end{itemize}
Each raises \texttt{emptyPath} when $Q$ has no point, and
\texttt{negativeDistance} when $d < 0$.

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
