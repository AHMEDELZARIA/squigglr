\section{MIS of the Edit History Module (\mref{mHistory})}
\label{sec:mHistory}

\subsection{Module}

EditHistory

\subsection{Uses}

None.

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

HistoryT is an abstract data type that keeps the drawing states needed to undo
and redo changes. Each state is a DrawingT of the Creative Drawing Module
(\mref{mCreative}).

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
new HistoryT & $x$: DrawingT & HistoryT & -- \\
record & $h$: HistoryT, $x$: DrawingT & -- & -- \\
undo & $h$: HistoryT & DrawingT & noUndo \\
redo & $h$: HistoryT & DrawingT & noRedo \\
canUndo & $h$: HistoryT & $\mathbb{B}$ & -- \\
canRedo & $h$: HistoryT & $\mathbb{B}$ & -- \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

None at the module level. Each HistoryT holds an ordered sequence of drawing
states and the position of the current state in that sequence.

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

None.

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item The Creative Drawing Module (\mref{mCreative}) records each completed
change once, after it has taken effect.
\item A new change after an undo discards the states that had been available
for redo.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

\texttt{record} appends the new drawing state; 
\texttt{undo} and \texttt{redo} move one state backward
or forward and return it for restoration. \texttt{canUndo} and \texttt{canRedo}
tell whether those operations are available. History is kept for the open
drawing only.

\subsubsection{Local Functions}

None.
