\section{MIS of the Refinement Engine Module (\mref{mRefineEngine})}
\label{sec:mRefineEngine}

\subsection{Module}

RefinementEngine

\subsection{Uses}

None.

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
refine & $P$: seq of PathT & seq of PathT & refinementFailed, unavailable \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

None.

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{service}$: the external refinement service, or its local
replacement.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item $P$ contains paths from one creative drawing, in stroke order and at the
scale of the drawing area.
\item The caller sends coordinates only, without learner or account
information (SAF-SHA-002).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

\texttt{refine} sends
the input paths to the refinement service and returns adjusted paths at the
same scale. It reports failure or unavailability rather than substituting a
successful looking result. The Refinement Control Module
(\mref{mRefineControl}) checks the result before applying it.
