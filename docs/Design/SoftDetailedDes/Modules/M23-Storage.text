\section{MIS of the Storage Module (\mref{mStorage})}
\label{sec:mStorage}

\subsection{Module}

Storage

\subsection{Uses}

Audit Log Module (\mref{mLog}).

\subsection{Syntax}

\subsubsection{Exported Constants}

None.

\subsubsection{Exported Types}

{\sloppy
\begin{itemize}
\item KindT $= \{\mathit{learner},$ $\mathit{settings},$ $\mathit{lesson},$
$\mathit{lessonRecord},$ $\mathit{attemptRecord},$ $\mathit{drawing}\}$, the
kinds of saved item. Each kind is laid out by the module that owns it: the
Learner Profile (\mref{mProfile}), Learner Settings (\mref{mSettings}), Lesson
Catalogue (\mref{mCatalogue}), Attempt Record (\mref{mRecord}) and Creative
Drawing (\mref{mCreative}) Modules.
\item ItemIdT, which identifies one saved item. No two items have the same
identifier.
\item ItemT, the data of one item, as its owner module lays it out: a tuple
whose fields are numbers, Booleans, strings, date-times, or sequences and
tuples of these. This module does not look inside an item, except to check its
text before saving (NFR-SEC-006).
\item EntryT = tuple of $(\mathit{id}: \text{ItemIdT},$ $\mathit{learner}:
\text{LearnerIdT} \cup \{\bot\},$ $\mathit{item}: \text{ItemT})$, one saved
item with the learner it belongs to.
\end{itemize}
}

\subsubsection{Exported Access Programs}

\begin{accessprograms}
newId & -- & ItemIdT & -- \\
put & $a$: AdultIdT, $k$: KindT, $p$: LearnerIdT $\cup$ $\{\bot\}$,
$\mathit{id}$: ItemIdT, $x$: ItemT & -- & notOwner, unchangeable, invalidInput,
unavailable \\
get & $a$: AdultIdT $\cup$ $\{\bot\}$, $k$: KindT, $\mathit{id}$: ItemIdT &
ItemT & notOwner, unknownItem, unavailable \\
list & $a$: AdultIdT $\cup$ $\{\bot\}$, $k$: KindT, $p$: LearnerIdT $\cup$
$\{\bot\}$ & seq of EntryT & notOwner, unavailable \\
remove & $a$: AdultIdT, $k$: KindT, $\mathit{id}$: ItemIdT & -- & notOwner,
unknownItem, unavailable \\
\end{accessprograms}

\subsection{Semantics}

\subsubsection{State Variables}

None.

\subsubsection{Environment Variables}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{db}$: the hosted database. It holds every saved item with its
kind, adult and learner, and the access rules that let an adult reach only
their own items (NFR-SEC-001, SAF-SHA-001).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
\item $a$ is the adult that the Adult Access Module (\mref{mAccess}) gives as
signed in.
\item The Attempt Record Module replaces a lesson record only until it ends
that record's lesson (FR-REC-009).
\item Lessons are stored by the team, outside \progname{}.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Access Routine Semantics}

The full semantics are given in Revision~0. In brief:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{newId} gives an identifier that no item has.
\item \texttt{put} saves $x$ as item $\mathit{id}$ of kind $k$, for adult $a$
and learner $p$, creating it or replacing it. $p = \bot$ is for an item that
belongs to the adult rather than to one learner, such as a learner profile.
\texttt{put} returns only after the server confirms the save (SAF-SHA-003).
Repeating a \texttt{put} with the same $\mathit{id}$ and $x$ changes nothing,
so an attempt that is sent again is still saved once (FR-REC-008).
\item A saved attempt record is never replaced: \texttt{put} with a different
$x$ raises \texttt{unchangeable} and logs the refused change with the Audit Log
Module, without the record's contents (FR-REC-009, NFR-SEC-003).
\item \texttt{get} and \texttt{list} give only $a$'s items (NFR-SEC-001).
\texttt{list} gives the items of learner $p$, or every item of kind $k$ that
belongs to $a$ when $p = \bot$. Lessons belong to no adult, so they are read
with $a = \bot$.
\item \texttt{remove} deletes an item. Who may delete records is still an open
issue in the SRS (OI-02).
\end{itemize}
\texttt{notOwner} is raised when $p$ or the item is not $a$'s, when $a = \bot$
for a kind other than $\mathit{lesson}$, and when \texttt{put} or
\texttt{remove} is given kind $\mathit{lesson}$. \texttt{invalidInput} is raised
when the text of $x$ fails the input check (NFR-SEC-006), and
\texttt{unavailable} when the server does not answer or does not confirm.

None.
