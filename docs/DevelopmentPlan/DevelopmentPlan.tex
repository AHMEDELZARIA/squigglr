\subsection{Shared Responsibilities}
\label{sec:shared-responsibilities}

The following responsibilities apply to every member, regardless of
their lead roles:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Software development:}
    Implement and maintain product features, resolve defects, and
    contribute to integration. Every member will receive substantive
    product-code tasks. Members may work across frontend, backend,
    and other technical areas with support from the relevant leads.

    \item \textbf{Testing:}
    Develop and run appropriate tests for their changes, investigate
    failures, and address defects. The Testing Lead coordinates the
    overall approach; each developer remains responsible for testing
    their own contributions.

    \item \textbf{Research and requirements:}
    Investigate stakeholder needs, domain knowledge, and technical
    approaches. Record supporting sources and assumptions, and
    contribute to defining and reviewing requirements.

    \item \textbf{Documentation:}
    Write and update the documentation associated with assigned work,
    including relevant requirements, design decisions, setup
    instructions, and test evidence.

    \item \textbf{Peer review:}
    Review teammates' code and documentation for correctness, clarity,
    and consistency with requirements and agreed standards. Each
    substantial change will be reviewed by someone other than its
    author before acceptance.

    \item \textbf{Task tracking and communication:}
    Keep assigned GitHub issues and project-board entries current,
    communicate progress and blockers, and notify the team when an
    agreed deadline is at risk.
\end{itemize}

Each substantive task will have a named owner, an agreed deadline, and
completion criteria recorded in a GitHub issue. Tasks involving multiple
components will also identify who is responsible for coordinating their
integration. These assignments establish individual accountability
within the team's shared responsibilities.

\subsection{Meeting and Milestone Responsibilities}
\label{sec:meeting-milestone-responsibilities}

Ahmed will chair team meetings as Project Manager, with a substitute
identified when he is unavailable. The chair will prepare the agenda,
guide discussion, and confirm action owners and deadlines. The notetaker
will rotate each meeting among all five members and will record
attendance, decisions, action items, and unresolved questions. The chair
and notetaker will be different members, with both assignments recorded
in the meeting issue.

A milestone Quality Assurance (QA) coordinator will rotate each
milestone among all five members. The coordinator will be named in the
milestone's tracking issue and will ensure that its deliverables are
reviewed against the applicable course checklists and Avenue rubric.
The coordinator will assign review actions, track corrections, and
verify that outstanding findings are resolved before submission.
Another member will review the coordinator's own contributions.

Milestone QA covers the completeness and consistency of the entire
deliverable, including documentation and submission requirements.
Huzaifah's ongoing Testing Lead role covers the software testing
strategy and coordination of test activities. All members remain
responsible for the quality of their own work under both arrangements.

\subsection{Workload Balance and Role Changes}
\label{sec:workload-role-changes}

At the start of each milestone, the team will allocate tasks using
estimated effort, member availability, and project priorities.
Coordination, research, review, and liaison duties will be included
when assessing workload. Members will support areas with heavier
implementation demands, and the Project Manager will coordinate
redistribution when tasks become blocked or workloads become uneven.
Shamil and Huzaifah will receive product-feature implementation tasks
alongside their DevOps, liaison, and testing responsibilities.

Lead assignments will be reviewed at each milestone and whenever
availability, interests, or project needs change. Any member may
request a reassignment during a team meeting or through a GitHub issue.
The team will agree on changes using the decision-making process in
the Team Charter and record the outcome in the meeting notes.

The outgoing lead will hand over current progress, outstanding actions,
known issues, and relevant resources to the incoming lead.
Table~\ref{tab:team-member-roles} and affected issue assignments will
then be updated. If a lead becomes unexpectedly unavailable, the team
will designate a temporary substitute and redistribute urgent work.

Members will share technical knowledge through repository documentation,
peer review, and brief walkthroughs. This overlap will help members
support one another and assume new responsibilities throughout the
project.
