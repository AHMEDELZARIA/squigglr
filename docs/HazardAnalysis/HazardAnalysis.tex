\subsection*{Rayan Nasrallah}

\textbf{1. What went well?}
The critical assumptions (CA-001 to CA-006) gave every hazard a stated cause. Each H-RAY row maps to a SAF-RAY requirement in the SRS, as the TA asked in the 7 October meeting. This made the mitigations traceable.

\textbf{2. Pain points and how I resolved them}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Product decisions changed while I was writing.} For example, the child no longer navigates lessons. I reworked CA-001, H-RAY-004/005 and SAF-RAY-005/006 to match.
  \item \emph{Unsourced wording.} Some early drafts had details nobody had discussed. I removed those and cited the interview where a cause came from it.
  \item \emph{Revision-history conflicts with main.} I fixed these with a merge.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{3. Which risks were known before, and which came up while writing?}
\textbf{[Rayan: confirm which were known before.]} Likely known before: lost or unsaved attempts on unreliable browsers or devices (H-RAY-002), and records read as a diagnosis (H-RAY-003), since the problem statement already avoids diagnosis. Found while writing: a child reaching adult-only actions when no adult is watching (H-RAY-001); repeated failures causing distress (H-RAY-004) and lessons restarted straight after the limit (H-RAY-005); the wrong child's records on a shared device (H-RAY-006); and a threshold set at a value that doesn't suit the child (H-RAY-007). These came from writing the critical assumptions and asking what happens when each one fails, together with the interview (for example muting audio, P2 37:39) and the supervisor's advice on configurable thresholds.

\textbf{4. Two other types of risk besides physical harm}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Privacy and security.} Squigglr stores records of children's attempts. Exposing them, or mixing them between children, harms the child and the family's trust (H-RAY-006).
  \item \emph{Data integrity and availability.} Lost or wrong records mislead the adults who rely on them to choose a child's level (H-RAY-002).
  \item \emph{Psychological well-being.} Frustration or a wrong conclusion about a child can do real harm without any physical injury (H-RAY-003, H-RAY-004).
\end{itemize}
