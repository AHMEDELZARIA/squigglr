\section{Mandated Constraints}
This section lists constraints imposed on Squigglr from outside the team:
by the supervisor, the teaching assistant (TA), the domain specialists
interviewed (SRC-005), the course and the university. They are not design choices. Each constraint has an identifier
of the form CON-\#\#\#, a description, a rationale, the party that
imposed it, a fit criterion that shows whether it is met, and its sources from the Requirement Sources
register (SRC-\#\#\#). The constraints limit what the team may build; they
do not choose a technology, library or design. For example, CON-001 requires
a website but does not name a framework. Table~\ref{tab:constraints}
summarizes them.

\subsection{Solution Constraints}
The supervisor, the TA and the domain specialists set the form of the
solution.

\begin{description}
\item[CON-001] Squigglr shall be a website used in a web browser.\\
\emph{Rationale:} Installing apps on school devices is difficult, and for
Canadian schools a website is much easier to adopt than an app-store app.\\
\emph{Imposed by:} supervisor and TA.\\
\emph{Fit criterion:} Squigglr opens and runs from a web address in a browser.\\
\emph{Source:} SRC-003, SRC-004.
\item[CON-002] Squigglr shall require no installation on the user's
device.\\
\emph{Rationale:} Shared school devices are often managed by the school,
so adults may not be able to install software. Opening a web address is
enough to start.\\
\emph{Imposed by:} supervisor and TA.\\
\emph{Fit criterion:} a first-time user starts an activity without installing any
app, plug-in or browser extension.\\
\emph{Source:} SRC-003, SRC-004.
\item[CON-003] Squigglr shall work on touchscreen devices with stylus
input.\\
\emph{Rationale:} Squigglr targets visual-motor skills, which include fine
motor control. Children build that control by practising with a held tool,
moving from large tools to smaller ones like pencils (SRC-005, P1, 26:46),
and how a child grips the tool affects how they draw (SRC-005, P3, 25:36).
Finger input is a stretch goal (SRC-001).\\
\emph{Imposed by:} domain specialists, within the scope agreed with the
supervisor and TA.\\
\emph{Fit criterion:} each activity can be completed with a stylus on a
touchscreen device; the device and browser combinations are TBD.\\
\emph{Source:} SRC-001, SRC-003, SRC-004, SRC-005.
\item[CON-007] Squigglr shall not decide when a child moves to another
stage or level; an adult chooses.\\
\emph{Rationale:} Deciding when a child is ready needs an assessment, which
is too complex for the project's time frame. The parent or educator, who
knows the child's level, should make that choice (SRC-005, P1, 28:37).
Squigglr may suggest going back a stage, but an adult must accept (G.3).\\
\emph{Imposed by:} domain specialists.\\
\emph{Fit criterion:} no change of stage or level happens without an
adult choosing or accepting it.\\
\emph{Source:} SRC-005.
\end{description}

\subsection{Anticipated Workplace Environment}
Squigglr will be used at home, in classrooms and in other places where
children practise drawing, such as rehabilitation centres (SRC-005, P2,
21:25), usually with an adult nearby. School devices may be shared by
several children (SRC-004). These places give context for later
requirements; they are not a constraint, since the team will not test
Squigglr in each of them.

\subsection{Enterprise Constraints}
The supervisor and the university set rules that protect children.

\begin{description}
\item[CON-005] The project shall gather no information from or about
children for requirements or evaluation.\\
\emph{Rationale:} The users are children, and the supervisor directed that
no information about them be collected. Requirements come from adults and
personas, and every check uses prepared or adult-made attempts. This
constraint covers the project's work; how Squigglr itself protects the
practice records it keeps is a privacy requirement, not this constraint.\\
\emph{Imposed by:} supervisor.\\
\emph{Fit criterion:} no requirement source, test or evaluation record
contains data from or about a child.\\
\emph{Source:} SRC-004.
\item[CON-006] The project shall run no study involving children.\\
\emph{Rationale:} Any study run through the university that involves
children requires ethics board approval, which the project does not seek
(SRC-005, P2, 22:56; P1, 33:00). Evaluation is done with adults only
(CON-005).\\
\emph{Imposed by:} supervisor and the university, as the domain
specialists also advised.\\
\emph{Fit criterion:} no study with child participants takes place during
the project.\\
\emph{Source:} SRC-004, SRC-005.
\end{description}
